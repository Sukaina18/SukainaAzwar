Project profile · Founder project

SafeNova

An AI- and blockchain-powered personal safety app designed to help people detect danger, report unsafe zones, and reach support quickly.

Role

Founder & Developer

Platform

iOS & Android

Status

Under discussion for national deployment

What I built

SafeNova brings together real-time safety tools and secure data handling in one cross-platform application. The concept is under active discussion with the IGP Police for national deployment.

AI-powered awareness

Danger detection designed to support faster awareness in potentially unsafe situations.

Connected alerts

Voice-triggered alerts, live video streaming with microphone access, and live location tracking.

Community reporting

Tools for reporting danger zones and helping build a more informed safety network.

Secure foundation

Blockchain-secured data combined with a MySQL-backed application architecture.

Technology

Flutter for the cross-platform mobile experience, Node.js for application services, AI for danger detection, blockchain for data security, and MySQL for data storage.
